Using the worldline quantum field theory formalism, we compute the radiation-reacted impulse, scattering angle, radiated energy and recoil of a classical black hole (or neutron star) scattering event at fifth post-Minkowskian and sub-leading self-force orders (5PM-1SF). This four-loop computation is considerably more challenging than the conservative 5PM-1SF counterpart. In the radiation sector, Calabi–Yau three-fold periods appear and contribute to the radiated energy and recoil observables.
