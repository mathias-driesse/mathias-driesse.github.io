We compute the 5PM order contributions to the scattering angle and impulse of classical black hole scattering in the conservative sector at first self-force order (1SF) using the worldline quantum field theory formalism. This four-loop computation required advanced integration-by-parts and differential equation technology implemented on high-performance computing systems. The function space turned out simpler than expected — only multiple polylogarithms up to weight three, and no elliptic integrals.
