My second PhD paper is out on arXiv: [*High-precision black hole scattering with Calabi–Yau manifolds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11846), co-authored with Gustav Jakobsen, Albrecht Klemm, Gustav Mogull, Christoph Nega, Benjamin Sauer, Johann Usovitsch, and my supervisor Jan Plefka.

This time, instead of the conservative dynamics, we computed the full dissipative dynamics at fifth order in Newton's constant $G$. Much harder than the conservative case — we found a new class of functions in the answer that had never appeared in quantum field theory before: *Calabi–Yau periods*, closely related to the Calabi–Yau manifolds that show up in string theory. We enlisted the help of Albrecht Klemm, a real expert in these objects, to make sense of them.
