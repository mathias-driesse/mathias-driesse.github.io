My first paper is out on arXiv: [*Conservative Black Hole Scattering at Fifth Post-Minkowskian and First Self-Force Order*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07781), written together with Gustav Jakobsen, Gustav Mogull, Benjamin Sauer, Johann Usovitsch, and my supervisor Jan Plefka.

The popular version: ever wondered how, when we detect gravitational waves, we know precisely how far away they came from and how massive the black holes were? This only works thanks to high-precision theoretical calculations — and they only get harder the more precise you push them. Future detectors like [LISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_Interferometer_Space_Antenna) will measure more accurately than the current state of the art can compute. We broke through several barriers to obtain the first fifth-order conservative post-Minkowskian terms using our *worldline quantum field theory* technique, leveraging insights from particle physics for the general-relativistic two-body problem.
