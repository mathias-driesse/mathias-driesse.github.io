I'm immensely proud to announce that our last paper, [*High-precision black hole scattering with Calabi–Yau manifolds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11846) has been accepted for publication in the journal *Nature* as part of the May 15th issue.

I'm even more proud that a visualization of some of our group's past work, namely the leading-order waveform, has ended up being featured on the cover of *Nature*! Holding a physical copy with that on the front was such a satisfying moment, I don't know if it can ever be topped.

We also have plenty of popular science coverage, most notably [*Scientific American*](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/black-hole-mergers-show-strange-mathematical-link-to-string-theory/). Go give them a read!
