My second paper of my PhD came out today on the arxiv, entitled "High-precision black hole scattering with Calabi-Yau manifolds", and co-authored with Gustav Jakobsen, Albrecht Klemm, Gustav Mogull, Christoph Nega, Benjamin Sauer, Johann Usovitsch, and my supervisor Jan Plefka. 

This time, instead of the conservative dynamics, we computed the full dissipative dynamics at fifth order in Newton's constant $G$. This was much more challenging than the previous case -- since we found a new class of functions in our answer that had never been put to use in quantum field theory before. These functions are known as Calabi-Yau periods and we had to enlist the help of a real expert on these, Albrecht Klemm, who has been learning about these functions, which are closely related to Calabi-Yau manifolds which appear in string theory.
